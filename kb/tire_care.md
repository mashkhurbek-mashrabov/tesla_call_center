## Tire Rotation and Maintenance

Tesla recommends rotating tires every 6,250 miles, or sooner if tread depth difference between tires reaches 2/32 of an inch or greater.

Tire rotation is a common Mobile Service appointment and can usually be performed at a location of the customer's choosing rather than at a Service Center.

Tire wear on electric vehicles can be faster than on comparable gasoline vehicles because of higher vehicle weight and instant torque delivery. Regular rotation and correct inflation extend tire life considerably.

## Tire Pressure

Recommended cold tire pressure is listed on the door pillar placard and in the Owner's Manual, and varies by model and wheel size.

Tire pressure drops in cold weather. The tire pressure warning frequently appears on the first cold morning of the season and often resolves after inflating the tires to the recommended cold pressure.

Check pressures when the tires are cold, meaning the vehicle has been parked for at least three hours or driven less than one mile.

## Flat Tires and Spare Tires

Tesla vehicles are not equipped with a spare tire and do not have the jacking provisions for a roadside tire change by the owner.

For a flat tire, contact Roadside Assistance through the Tesla app. Depending on location and availability, Roadside Assistance will arrange a temporary loaner wheel or tow the vehicle to the nearest Service Center or Tesla-approved tire shop.

## Tire Repair and Warranty Coverage

Tires are a wear item and are not covered by the Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty. Tire repair and replacement costs are the owner's responsibility.

Tires may carry a separate limited warranty from the tire manufacturer. Road hazard coverage, where it exists, typically comes from the tire manufacturer or a separate road hazard policy rather than from Tesla.

## Wheel Alignment

Uneven or accelerated tire wear often indicates a wheel alignment issue. An alignment check can be requested through the Tesla app as a service appointment.

Alignment is generally not a warranty item unless it results from a covered defect, so an estimate is provided for approval before the work is performed.
