## Customer Support Phone Number

Tesla Customer Support can be reached at 877-798-3752. The vehicle support line is available Monday through Saturday, 4 AM to 9 PM Pacific Time.

## Phone Menu Options

For Powerwall and solar support, call 877-798-3752 and select option 4, then option 2.

For Wall Connector and other home charging products, call 877-798-3752 and select option 3.

## Roadside Assistance Line

Roadside Assistance is available through the Tesla app, which shares vehicle location automatically, or by phone at 877-798-3752. Roadside Assistance is available 24 hours a day for customers with active coverage.

## Support Through the Tesla App and Account

Most support requests are handled fastest through the Tesla app. Service requests, cost estimates, appointment scheduling, and messaging with a Service Center representative all happen in the app.

Customers signed in to a Tesla Account can also submit a written question through the support website. Without an account, the website lists the available contact options.

## Escalating to a Human Representative

If an issue cannot be resolved through the app or self-service options, a Customer Support representative can be reached on the phone line during published hours. For vehicle-specific matters, have the VIN and current mileage ready before calling.

## Additional Support Resources

Tesla publishes video guides covering vehicle and energy product features. The support site organizes topics including Home Charging, Do It Yourself Guides, Schedule a Service Visit, Find a Collision Center, Roadside Assistance, Vehicle Warranty, Tire Care and Repair, Account Support, and the Tesla App.
