## Placing an Order

Vehicles are ordered online through the Tesla Design Studio. The order requires a non-refundable order fee of 250 dollars in the United States, and a destination and documentation fee of 1,390 dollars is added to the vehicle price.

After ordering, the order and its status are tracked in the Tesla Account and the Tesla app.

## Changing or Canceling an Order

The configuration can be changed in the Tesla Account until the vehicle enters production. Once the vehicle is matched to a VIN and enters final preparation, changes are no longer possible.

Changing the configuration usually resets the delivery estimate, because the order re-enters the queue for the new configuration.

Orders can be canceled from the Tesla Account. The order fee is generally non-refundable.

## Delivery Timing and VIN Assignment

Delivery estimates shown at order time are ranges, not fixed dates. The estimate narrows as the order progresses and becomes firm once a VIN is assigned.

A VIN is assigned shortly before delivery. Once assigned, the Tesla app shows the final paperwork, the amount due, and the scheduled delivery appointment.

## Preparing for Delivery Day

Complete all required steps in the Tesla app before the appointment: upload proof of insurance, complete financing or payment, provide registration details, and sign the required documents.

Bring a valid driver's license and, where required, the method of payment for any remaining balance. Delivery cannot be completed until payment has cleared and insurance is active.

## Inspecting the Vehicle at Delivery

Inspect the vehicle at delivery in good light. Check panel gaps and alignment, paint condition, glass, wheels, interior trim, and that all included accessories such as the Mobile Connector and charge adapters are present.

Note any cosmetic concerns in the Tesla app at the time of delivery. Concerns documented at delivery are handled as due-bill items by the Service Center.

## Trade-Ins and Financing

A trade-in can be added to an order in the Tesla Account. The trade-in offer is based on the information provided and is subject to inspection on delivery day.

Financing and leasing applications are completed in the Tesla Account. Approval is typically valid for a limited window, so applying too early in a long delivery timeline may require re-approval before delivery.

## Taking Delivery Outside the Original Market

A vehicle ordered in one market and transported to another may have a limited set of features and limited support availability in the new market.
