## What the Extended Service Agreement Is

The Extended Service Agreement, often shortened to ESA, is an optional subscription that extends coverage after the vehicle's factory warranty ends.

Coverage begins once the Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty or the Pre-Owned Vehicle Limited Warranty expires. It runs until the vehicle reaches 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first.

The agreement is billed as a monthly subscription fee rather than a single up-front purchase.

## What the Extended Service Agreement Includes

The Extended Service Agreement covers repair or replacement of covered components due to defects in materials or workmanship, in the same spirit as the Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty.

Tesla Roadside Assistance is included for the duration of the agreement and is available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

## What the Extended Service Agreement Does Not Cover

The Extended Service Agreement does not cover collision damage, cosmetic damage, or normal wear-and-tear items such as tires, brake pads, and wiper blades.

Damage from abuse, neglect, misuse, or from non-Tesla modifications and third-party charging equipment is excluded.

The high-voltage battery and drive unit are already covered separately by the Battery and Drive Unit Limited Warranty, which runs 8 years with a model-specific mileage cap.

## Eligibility and Enrollment

Eligibility depends on the vehicle's age, mileage, and current warranty status at the time of enrollment. A vehicle that has already passed 8 years or 100,000 miles cannot be enrolled, because the agreement's own coverage window would already be over.

Enrollment and cancellation are handled through the Tesla Account and the Tesla app.

## Where to Get Coverage Questions Answered

For questions about whether a specific repair is covered under warranty or under an Extended Service Agreement, contact the Service team directly through the Tesla app.

Certain repairs require an in-person inspection before coverage can be confirmed. These appear on the estimate with an initial cost, and if the repair qualifies, the charges are removed on the day of the appointment.
