## What the Tesla App Does

The Tesla app is the primary interface for owning and servicing a Tesla. It controls the vehicle remotely, manages charging, schedules service, and handles account and payment settings.

Remote features include locking and unlocking, climate preconditioning, opening the trunk and frunk, Summon, checking charge status, and locating the vehicle on a map.

## Signing In and Account Access

The Tesla app uses the same credentials as the Tesla Account on the website. Two-factor authentication is strongly recommended and can be enabled from the account security settings.

If a phone is lost or replaced, sign in on the new device with the same Tesla Account. The vehicle may prompt for the key card to re-authenticate the new phone as a Phone Key.

## Phone Key Setup and Troubleshooting

Phone Key uses Bluetooth to unlock and start the vehicle as the driver approaches. Setup requires signing in to the app, enabling Bluetooth, and tapping the key card to the center console when prompted.

If Phone Key stops working, confirm Bluetooth is on, confirm the app has background and location permissions, force-close and reopen the app, and toggle Bluetooth off and on. As a last resort, remove the phone as a key from the vehicle touchscreen and set it up again. The key card always works as a backup.

## Adding and Removing Drivers

Additional drivers are added through the app by sending an invitation to their Tesla Account email address. Each driver keeps their own profile, mirror and seat settings, and app access.

The vehicle owner can revoke a driver's access at any time from the app.

## Payment Methods and Charging History

The payment method on file in the Tesla Account is used for Supercharging sessions, idle and congestion fees, subscriptions, and service invoices.

Charging history, including the cost and energy delivered for each session, is available in the app under Charging. Invoices can be downloaded from the Tesla Account on the website.

## Subscriptions

Premium Connectivity, Full Self-Driving Supervised, and the Extended Service Agreement are managed as subscriptions in the Tesla Account. They can be started and canceled from the app.

Premium Connectivity provides satellite maps, live traffic visualization, video streaming, and music streaming over the vehicle's cellular connection. Standard Connectivity provides basic navigation and over-the-air updates over Wi-Fi.
