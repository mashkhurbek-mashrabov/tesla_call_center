## Current Lineup

Tesla sells three vehicles in 2026: Model 3, Model Y, and Cybertruck. Model S and Model X production ended in 2026, and any remaining new units are existing inventory rather than new factory orders.

## Model 3

Model 3 starts at 36,990 dollars before destination and order fees. With the 1,390 dollar destination charge and 250 dollar order fee, the entry configuration comes to roughly 38,630 dollars.

Model 3 is offered in four configurations: Standard Rear-Wheel Drive, Premium Rear-Wheel Drive, Premium All-Wheel Drive, and Performance. The trims previously called Long Range are now called Premium.

EPA-estimated range is 321 miles for Standard Rear-Wheel Drive, 363 miles for Premium Rear-Wheel Drive, 346 miles for Premium All-Wheel Drive, and 314 miles for Performance.

The Performance configuration accelerates from 0 to 60 miles per hour in 2.9 seconds with a top speed of 155 miles per hour. Its base price is approximately 56,630 dollars including destination and order fees.

The Standard trim is limited to rear-wheel drive, three paint colors, and one interior color. Ventilated front seats, heated rear seats, and the rear 8-inch touchscreen are reserved for higher trims. The Standard trim still includes Traffic-Aware Cruise Control, a front-mounted camera, a 30-day Full Self-Driving Supervised trial, and a 30-day Premium Connectivity trial.

## Model Y

Model Y starts at 39,990 dollars for the Standard Rear-Wheel Drive configuration. The 2026 refresh is known internally as Juniper.

The lineup spans six configurations, including two trim levels for each drivetrain, a Performance trim, and a new three-row variant.

EPA-estimated range is 321 miles for Standard Rear-Wheel Drive, 294 miles for Standard All-Wheel Drive, 357 miles for Premium Rear-Wheel Drive, 327 miles for Premium All-Wheel Drive, and 306 miles for Performance.

Model Y Performance starts at 58,880 dollars, accelerates from 0 to 60 miles per hour in 3.3 seconds, tops out at 155 miles per hour, and is EPA-rated at 306 miles on 21-inch wheels.

The entry Rear-Wheel Drive trim includes 18-inch wheels, LED headlights, a hands-free power liftgate, cloth seats with synthetic leather accents, heated power front seats, a heated steering wheel, a 15.4-inch touchscreen, seven speakers, and two wireless phone chargers.

## Model Y L Three-Row

Model Y L is a three-row variant added in July 2026 and built at Giga Texas. Orders opened in the United States on July 2, 2026, with first deliveries quoted for September 2026.

It is the roomiest Model Y and the only six-seat configuration. A seven-seat interior is available as a 2,500 dollar option.

## Cybertruck

Cybertruck starts at 69,990 dollars. On the used market Cybertruck typically ranges from 65,000 to 95,000 dollars or more for a Cyberbeast configuration.

Cybertruck carries the longer Battery and Drive Unit Limited Warranty of 8 years or 150,000 miles, matching Model S and Model X.

## Full Self-Driving and Charging Notes

Full Self-Driving Supervised is available on every 2026 Tesla. The one-time purchase option ended on February 14, 2026, and it is now subscription-only at 99 dollars per month in the United States.

Every current Tesla uses a native NACS charging port and connects to the Supercharger network without an adapter. Maximum DC fast-charging rates are up to 250 kilowatts for Premium, Performance, and Model Y L, and up to 225 kilowatts for Standard trims.

The federal EV tax credit has expired and is no longer available on new purchases.

Configurator pricing changes periodically. Current pricing should always be confirmed on the Tesla website before ordering.
