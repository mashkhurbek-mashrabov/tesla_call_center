## Powerwall Overview

Powerwall is a rechargeable home battery that stores energy from solar or from the grid and supplies the home during an outage or during expensive peak-rate hours.

Powerwall is monitored and controlled through the Tesla app, which shows live energy flow between solar, the battery, the home, and the grid.

## Powerwall Operating Modes

Self-Powered mode prioritizes using stored solar energy in the home rather than exporting it to the grid.

Time-Based Control charges and discharges the battery according to utility rate periods, storing energy when rates are low and using it when rates are high.

Backup Only mode keeps the battery fully charged and reserves it entirely for grid outages.

A Backup Reserve percentage sets how much capacity is held back for outages regardless of the selected mode.

## Powerwall During a Grid Outage

When the grid goes down, Powerwall disconnects the home from the grid and supplies backup power automatically, typically within a fraction of a second.

How long backup power lasts depends on the stored energy, the home's consumption, and whether solar is recharging the battery during daylight hours.

Solar alone cannot power a home during an outage for safety reasons. A Powerwall or equivalent battery is required for solar to continue operating when the grid is down.

## Solar Panels and Solar Roof

Tesla offers solar panels and Solar Roof for residential installation. System production is monitored in the Tesla app alongside Powerwall.

Solar systems include a manufacturer warranty on the panels and a separate workmanship warranty on the installation. Specific warranty terms depend on the product and the installation date.

## Wall Connector Support

The Wall Connector is a home charging product rather than an energy storage product, but it is supported through the energy product support channels.

For Wall Connector and other home charging product support, call 877-798-3752 and select option 3.

## Energy Product Support Contact

For Powerwall and solar support, call 877-798-3752 and select option 4, then option 2.

Support requests for energy products can also be submitted through the Tesla app or the Tesla Account on the website.
