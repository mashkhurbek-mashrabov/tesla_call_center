## What Roadside Assistance Covers

Tesla Roadside Assistance provides immediate help for issues that leave a vehicle unable to continue driving. Covered services include towing to the nearest Tesla Service Center, flat tire assistance, and vehicle lockout assistance.

Roadside Assistance is included for the duration of the Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty, which is 4 years from the vehicle's first delivery date.

Customers with an active Extended Service Agreement have Tesla Roadside Assistance available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year for the duration of that agreement.

## How to Request Roadside Assistance

The fastest way to request Roadside Assistance is through the Tesla app, which shares the vehicle's location automatically.

When requesting help, be ready to provide the VIN, the license plate number, current mileage, the vehicle's location, and a description of the problem.

Roadside Assistance can also be reached by phone at 877-798-3752.

## Roadside Assistance Exclusions

Roadside Assistance services may not be available in all situations, and where available may be subject to fees or charges with advance notice.

Common exclusions include transportation costs from the Service Center after repairs are completed, depletion of the high-voltage battery, low-voltage battery depletion resulting from normal wear and tear, and any incident arising from off-road driving or driving on hazardous surfaces.

## Locked Out of the Vehicle

If you are locked out of your vehicle and cannot get in, Roadside Assistance provides lockout service. Contact Roadside Assistance through the Tesla app or call 877-798-3752.

Before requesting lockout service, try unlocking from the Tesla app, which works over the vehicle's cellular connection and does not depend on being near the car. If the phone is dead or the app cannot reach the vehicle, the key card will unlock it when tapped against the door pillar.

If the phone key has stopped working but you can still reach the vehicle, the issue is usually Bluetooth or app permissions rather than a lockout. That is a phone key problem, not a roadside call.

Lockout assistance is covered for the duration of the Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty and for customers with an active Extended Service Agreement.

## Towing Guidance

Tesla vehicles must be transported by flatbed truck. Towing a Tesla with the wheels on the ground can cause serious damage to the drive units.

Before a tow, the vehicle may need to be placed into Transport Mode through the vehicle touchscreen so the parking brake releases and the vehicle can be winched onto the flatbed.

## Flat Tires

Tesla vehicles are not equipped with a spare tire. For a flat tire, Roadside Assistance will either arrange a temporary loaner wheel where available or tow the vehicle to the nearest Service Center or Tesla-approved tire shop.

Tire damage itself is a wear item and is not covered by the vehicle warranty, so the cost of tire repair or replacement is the owner's responsibility.
