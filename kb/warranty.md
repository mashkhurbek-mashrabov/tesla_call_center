## Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty

The Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty covers repair or replacement of any part manufactured or supplied by Tesla that is defective in materials or workmanship. It lasts 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first.

Coverage begins on the date the vehicle is first delivered to its original buyer, leased, or registered as a company car or demonstrator vehicle. During this period Tesla repairs or replaces covered defective parts at no charge to the owner.

This is essentially bumper-to-bumper coverage. The battery, drive unit, restraint system, and body rust have their own separate and longer warranties.

## What the Basic Warranty Does Not Cover

The Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty does not cover damage from collisions or accidents. It does not cover normal wear-and-tear items such as tires, seat covers, brake pads, and windshield wiper blades.

Damage caused by abuse, neglect, or misuse is excluded. Damage caused by third-party vehicle adaptors or third-party chargers is not covered.

Tesla reserves the right to deny warranty coverage where the vehicle or its components were damaged by abuse or negligence.

## Battery and Drive Unit Limited Warranty

The Battery and Drive Unit Limited Warranty runs 8 years, with a mileage cap that depends on the model.

Model S, Model X, and Cybertruck are covered for 8 years or 150,000 miles, whichever comes first. Model 3 Rear-Wheel Drive and Model Y Rear-Wheel Drive and All-Wheel Drive are covered for 8 years or 100,000 miles. Model 3 and Model Y Premium and Performance variants are covered for 8 years or 120,000 miles.

## Battery Capacity Retention Guarantee

Across every model, Tesla guarantees that the high-voltage battery retains at least 70 percent of its original capacity over the warranty period. If capacity falls below 70 percent while the Battery and Drive Unit Limited Warranty is still in effect, Tesla will repair or replace the battery.

## Restraint System and Body Rust Coverage

The restraint system, which includes seat belts and the airbag system, is covered for 5 years or 60,000 miles, whichever comes first.

Rust perforation caused by a manufacturing defect is covered for 12 years with no mileage limit, for vehicles purchased after February 1, 2019.

## Roadside Assistance Included With Warranty

Roadside Assistance is included for the duration of the Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty, which is 4 years. It covers services such as towing to the nearest Tesla Service Center and lockout assistance.

## Used Vehicle Warranty Coverage

If a used Tesla is purchased directly from Tesla, the remainder of the original 4-year or 50,000-mile Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty transfers to the new owner.

A Used Vehicle Limited Warranty of 1 year or 10,000 miles takes effect when the New Vehicle Limited Warranty expires, or on the purchase date if no new vehicle coverage remains.

Battery and Drive Unit coverage also transfers, and is calculated from the vehicle's original in-service date rather than the resale date.

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

To request a warranty repair, schedule a service appointment in the Tesla app. Provide the VIN, current mileage, and a description of the problem. The vehicle can be brought to a Service Center, or a Mobile Service technician may be dispatched depending on the type of repair.

Some repairs require an in-person inspection before coverage can be confirmed. These may appear on the estimate with an initial cost, and if the repair qualifies as a warranty repair, those charges are removed on the day of the appointment.
