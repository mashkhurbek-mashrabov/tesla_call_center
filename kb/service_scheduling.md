## Types of Service Appointments

Tesla offers three types of service appointments: Service Center visits, Mobile Service appointments, and Collision Center visits.

A Service Center visit requires bringing the vehicle to a Tesla Service location. A Mobile Service technician travels to a location of the customer's choosing and performs the work there. Collision Center visits handle body repairs after an accident.

The appointment types offered are determined by the type of service needed and the customer's location. Some services can only be completed at a Service Center, and some only through Mobile Service. Mobile Service typically covers simpler work such as tire rotations and cabin air filter replacements.

## How to Schedule a Service Appointment

Open the Tesla app, tap Service, then tap Request Service. Select a support topic and describe the concern, then follow the on-screen prompts.

If the concern requires an appointment, the app asks for additional information such as photos, insurance information where applicable, and a preferred date, time, and appointment type.

If the concern does not require an appointment, the app provides troubleshooting steps instead.

After booking, a Service Center representative may reach out through the app's messaging feature for clarification.

## Service Cost Estimates

All types of service appointments require the customer to review and confirm a cost estimate before any work is performed on the vehicle.

The service team pre-diagnoses the vehicle using remote diagnostics and orders any needed parts before the appointment. The customer then receives a notification to review the estimated cost.

To view an estimate, open the Tesla app, tap Service, then tap View Estimate. The estimate must be approved before work can begin, so approving it promptly avoids delays. Estimates can also be reviewed with the service team at the appointment.

## Rescheduling or Canceling an Appointment

Appointments are scheduled, rescheduled, and canceled through the Tesla app. The app shows nearby Service Centers with available dates, including Mobile Service options.

Additional services can be added to an already-scheduled appointment through the app.

## Preparing for a Mobile Service Appointment

Note all concerns in the Tesla app before the appointment. The technician reviews these notes in advance and brings the necessary tools and parts.

Notes can be changed up until 24 hours before the appointment. Changes after that point may mean the technician does not have the right parts on hand.

## Preparing for a Service Center Visit

Remove child seats and valuables from the vehicle, and clear the trunk and storage areas so technicians have easy access.

Bring the key fob or key card if the vehicle uses one.

Enable notifications in device settings, because estimates, confirmations, and other communication arrive through the Tesla app.

Drop-off and pickup can often occur outside the Service Center's opening hours, and alternative transportation may be available depending on the location and the repair.

## Service Outside the Original Market

A vehicle may be brought to a local Service Center for service, but transporting a vehicle outside its original market may result in a limited set of features and limited support availability.
